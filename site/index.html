Abstract

We answer negatively Ta's Problems 11.6–11.7 in arXiv:2505.08090v5, Section 11, page 27, and prove his Conjecture 5.13 in arXiv:2508.16772v1, Section 5.2.2, page 17, extending the counting methods of Kamada–Oshiro (2009), Ta (2025), and Nakajima–Yamaguchi (2026). The two semidirect products C16 semidirect C4 with actions 5 and 7 have invariant triple (64,4,22), but respectively 8847360 and 2160000 good involutions. Ta's central-label characterization gives a weighted-involution formula for every finite conjugation subquandle and an exact criterion for equality in the central-label bound. The quaternion example attains that bound with two orbits and a center of order two. Inversion-closure is distinguished from classwise inverse stability, correcting Remark 2.14. For the linear quandle Lambda_(4n,2n-1), the number of good involutions is ten for odd n and four for even n; the odd case proves Conjecture 5.13, and the even case reproves Ta's Remark 5.14.
